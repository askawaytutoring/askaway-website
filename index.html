About ASKaway

ASKaway Tutoring is affordable mathematic tutoring and instruction for elementary and middle school students, built on a simple idea: quality learning shouldn't come with a luxury price tag. For a fraction of the average tutoring cost in California, students get one-on-one academic support and the experience of students at the highest levels.

A small portion of each session covers materials and program costs. The rest is donated directly to UNICEF, which works in over 190 countries to protect children's rights, health, and education worldwide. Every session held locally helps fund that same mission on a global scale.

ASKaway started in founder Abhinav Kodali's sixth grade, when a Rubik's Cube hobby turned into free lessons for five elementary schoolers. That group grew to over thirty, competed together at their first official competition, and eventually became the foundation for something bigger: a tutoring program built to make real academic help accessible to any family that needs it, not just those who can afford it.

Whether your student wants support with their math or hopes to prepare for their next level of material, every session supports two communities at once — yours, and one much farther away.
